00 · Site

Staked out
before anything
is ordered.

Corner pegs, string lines, a soil report. The footprint goes on the ground first, and the drawings follow the ground rather than the other way round.

01 · Groundworks

Frozen ground,
opened properly.

The access road goes in before the excavator does, so no delivery ever sits waiting at the treeline. Frost is thawed under blankets, not fought with a bigger bucket.

02 · Foundation

Concrete that
cures at minus
twelve.

Insulated formwork, heated cure blankets, and a mix designed for the cold. We pour in January because the ground is hard and the site stays clean.

03 · Frame

Two storeys
in nine days.

Spruce studs are cut and numbered in our shop, then stood straight off the truck. Nothing is measured twice in the snow.

04 · Roof

Closed before
the next
snowfall.

Trusses are craned in the same week the frame tops out. Standing-seam steel follows within seventy-two hours of the ridge going up.

05 · Envelope

Sealed, taped,
and glazed.

Triple glazing, a continuous taped membrane, four hundred millimetres of cellulose. Every house is blower-door tested before the cladding hides anything.

06 · Cladding

Charred spruce,
burnt by hand.

Vertical boards, flame-treated and oiled in our yard. It weathers to graphite and will not ask for a coat of paint in your lifetime.

07 · Handover

Lights on.

Fourteen weeks from the first peg. You get the keys, the full set of drawings, and the name of every person who touched the house.

Method

Three things that make
winter the cheap season.

01

The frame is cut indoors

Every stud, header and truss is machined in a heated shop and arrives numbered. The crew on site assembles; it does not fabricate.

02

Hard ground is good ground

Frozen soil carries a tracked excavator without churning. The plot you hand back in spring looks like the plot you started with.

03

One crew, start to finish

The same eight people are there for the pegs and for the handover. No subcontractor inherits a problem they did not create.

What is in every

Glazing

Roof
Standing-seam steel, 50-year finish warranty
Cladding

Heating
